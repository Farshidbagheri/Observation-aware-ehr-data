# Figure guide and scientific interpretation

All figures are generated by `scripts/publication_atlas.py` and `scripts/source_inventory.py` from the public [MIMIC-IV-ED Demo 2.2](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-ed-demo/2.2/) and [MIMIC-IV Clinical Demo 2.2](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-demo/2.2/). They describe **documentation patterns** in a selected small sample. They are not population estimates, clinical recommendations, or validation of a prediction model.

## Observation coverage

**01 — Observation matrix** ([PNG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/01_observation_atlas.png), [SVG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/01_observation_atlas.svg))  
Unit: ED stays with at least one recorded vital-field measurement during a specified interval after ED arrival. Denominator: ED stays whose duration extends to the start of the interval (222, 221, 221, 210, 170 and 63, respectively); not all stays at all times. Cells with fewer than ten recorded stays are masked. *A missing recording does not establish absence of physiology or clinical attention.*

**02 — Source-field completeness** ([PNG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/02_completeness_intervals.png), [SVG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/02_completeness_intervals.svg))  
Unit: records in `vitalsign` (1,038 source rows), with a nonblank field. Wilson 95% intervals describe a **simple binomial row model**; repeated records from 64 ED patients are correlated, so these intervals are **not calibrated patient-level uncertainty intervals** or external-population confidence limits. Interpret the point estimates descriptively only. Patient-cluster resampling is a needed follow-up for formal uncertainty.

**03 — Temporal recording intensity** ([PNG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/03_temporal_recording.png), [SVG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/03_temporal_recording.svg))  
Unit: eligible ED stays with at least one source record in a time interval. The three series reflect Pyxis dispensing records and matched hospital `labevents` and `emar` records. **No curve measures actual antibiotic administration or clinician observation.** Low-count cells are suppressed.

## Event timing and linkage

**04 — ED encounter duration** ([PNG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/04_encounter_duration.png), [SVG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/04_encounter_duration.svg))  
Unit: ED encounter, with duration calculated from `outtime - intime`, displayed as ECDF and boxplot. Sample size: 222 stays.

**05 — Lab chart-to-store timestamp lag** ([PNG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/05_lab_recording_lag.png), [SVG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/05_lab_recording_lag.svg))  
Unit: hospital `labevents` source row with both timestamps and nonnegative `storetime - charttime`. Missing and contradictory timestamps are separately counted in the aggregate JSON; the histogram truncation and ECDF axis limits must not be mistaken for complete observation ranges. *Storage does not equal clinician viewing.*

**06 — Cross-source linkage** ([PNG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/06_cross_source_linkage.png), [SVG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/06_cross_source_linkage.svg))  
Unit: **hospital records**, not patients. Join criteria: matching `subject_id`, matching nonempty `hadm_id`, and chart timestamp inside an ED stay. Linked rows: 1,833 laboratory and 595 eMAR records. Unmatched counts are not treatment failures, clinical negative outcomes, or exclusively data-quality problems.

**07 — Source schema fingerprint** ([PNG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/07_schema_fingerprint.png), [SVG](../evidence/publication_atlas/figures/07_schema_fingerprint.svg))  
Unit: source CSV table. Displays whether key identifier/time columns exist and their nonmissing fraction across all 37 source files. Grey denotes a column that does not belong to that table's schema, not missing clinical information. The inventory includes reference and terminology tables, so total record counts must not be described as independent patient observations.
